An automated brand protection platform that hunts counterfeit product listings across the open web and files DMCA takedowns. Sweeps brand imagery through reverse image search spanning the web, marketplaces and social platforms, scraping candidates with stealth browsers that bypass anti-bot systems. Confirmed infringements clear a five-signal image verification filter combining perceptual hashing, color histograms and SIFT, then get enriched with RDAP domain intelligence and packaged as ready-to-file DMCA cases.
